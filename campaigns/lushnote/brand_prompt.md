Create a 1:1 square Facebook post for LushNote, 1080 × 1080 px.

LushNote is a clinical documentation and note-taking app for doctors and clinicians in Australia.

The post must look like a professionally designed Canva/Figma social-media advertisement created by a human graphic designer.

The visual identity must remain recognisably LushNote, but the composition must NOT repeat the same template from post to post.

LushNote

The design should feel like a high-quality Canva template that has been manually customised by a competent designer.

Desired qualities:

- clean
- editorial
- simple
- confident
- contemporary
- approachable
- healthcare-appropriate
- Australian
- visually restrained
- easy to understand immediately
- professionally typeset
- intentionally composed

The design must NOT look like a stereotypical AI-generated advertisement.

The central principle is:

ONE POST.
ONE IDEA.
ONE DOMINANT VISUAL DECISION.

Creativity should come from:
- composition
- typography
- cropping
- proportion
- negative space
- visual contrast
- positioning
- scale
- photography choice
- simple shape interaction

not from excessive decorative elements.

======================================================================
CAMPAIGN-BASED DESIGN LOGIC
======================================================================

Interpret the campaign before composing the artwork.

If the campaign is a SHORT, PUNCHY STATEMENT:
Prefer typography as the dominant visual.

Examples of suitable treatment:
- huge headline
- substantial empty space
- minimal supporting copy
- one flat shape or line treatment
- possibly no photograph

If the campaign is about a PRODUCT FEATURE:
Prefer the actual LushNote interface or supplied screenshot.

Possible treatments:
- one clean straight-on phone
- enlarged cropped interface
- screenshot alongside typography
- screenshot occupying most of the composition
- interface partially cropped intentionally

Do not create fictional UI.

If the campaign is about a RELATABLE CLINICIAN MOMENT:
Prefer one believable editorial photograph.

Examples:
- coffee during a break
- workstation after clinic
- doctor typing
- papers on a desk
- quiet staff room
- hospital corridor
- clinician leaving work
- clinical desk still-life

The photograph should support the idea rather than literally illustrate every word.

If the campaign is PROMOTIONAL:
A stronger colour field is allowed.

Use:
- deep blue background
- white headline
- mint CTA
- restrained bright-blue accent

Keep it simple.

Do not turn the promotion into a price-tag collage.

If the campaign asks a QUESTION:
The question itself can dominate the canvas.

Responses or options may appear as:
- simple outlined rectangles
- plain text choices
- minimal poll-style elements

Do not turn them into colourful floating cards.

If the campaign addresses MULTIPLE SPECIALTIES or USE CASES:
Prefer a strong headline and one neutral healthcare visual rather than creating an icon for every specialty.

If the campaign is EDUCATIONAL:
Use editorial hierarchy rather than infographic overload.

If the campaign is EMOTIONAL or HUMAN:
Use photography or extremely simple typography.

Avoid visual clichés.

======================================================================
TYPOGRAPHY
======================================================================

Typography is one of the primary visual assets of the LushNote brand.

Use a modern sans-serif family.

Headline style:
- bold to extra-bold
- clean
- strong
- compact
- similar in visual character to Poppins, Montserrat, League Spartan, Archivo, or another contemporary geometric sans-serif

The LUSHNOTE wordmark may use a narrower condensed sans-serif treatment consistent with the existing campaign references.

Use no more than:
- one primary type family
- two or three font weights
- three major text sizes

The headline should create the visual hierarchy.

Supporting copy must clearly sit below it in importance.

Avoid making every line bold.

Avoid excessive uppercase.

Do not randomly change fonts between words.

Do not highlight random words with unrelated colours.

Do not use:
- handwriting fonts
- brush lettering
- script fonts
- novelty fonts
- futuristic sci-fi fonts
- distressed fonts

unless explicitly requested by the campaign.

Do not use decorative typography merely to make the design look creative.

======================================================================
HEADLINE TREATMENT
======================================================================

The supplied headline wording must remain exact.

However, creatively determine:
- line breaks
- relative scale
- placement
- alignment
- whether the headline occupies one-third, one-half or most of the canvas
- whether one line becomes dramatically larger
- whether the headline interacts with the photograph
- whether the headline is centred, left aligned or occasionally right aligned

Do not default to exactly two headline lines.

Choose line breaks for visual rhythm and readability.

Never distort letters.

Never use warped text.

======================================================================
NEGATIVE SPACE
======================================================================

Empty space is an intentional design element.

Do not fill every available area.

A substantial portion of the composition may remain completely empty.

Approximately 25–50% breathing room is acceptable when appropriate.

A minimal post containing:
logo + headline + one sentence + one simple shape

can be more effective than a complicated post.

Do not add something merely because an area looks empty.

======================================================================
GRID AND ALIGNMENT
======================================================================

Use an underlying professional grid.

Elements should appear deliberately related.

Maintain:
- clean margins
- consistent text edges
- clear visual alignment
- adequate separation between headline and body
- adequate logo clear space
- comfortable spacing from canvas edges

Do not make the layout excessively mathematically symmetrical.

Slight controlled asymmetry is encouraged when it improves the composition.

Avoid the generic AI tendency of placing every object perfectly centred inside a rounded card.

======================================================================
QUALITY CHECK BEFORE FINALISING
======================================================================

Before producing the final image, internally evaluate the composition against these questions:

1. Is there one obvious focal point?
2. Can the main message be understood quickly in a Facebook feed?
3. Is there enough negative space?
4. Does the composition look manually designed rather than algorithmically decorated?
5. Is anything present purely to fill space?
6. Are there too many graphic elements?
7. Is the photography believable?
8. Does the app interface match supplied material?
9. Does the branding clearly belong to LushNote?
10. Does this layout differ meaningfully from the previous LushNote campaign examples while retaining the same visual identity?

If the design feels busy, remove elements.

If it feels generically AI-generated, simplify it substantially.
