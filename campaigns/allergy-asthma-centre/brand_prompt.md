======================================================================
ABSOLUTE MEDICAL COMMUNICATION RULES
======================================================================

Never write, imply or visually suggest a guaranteed cure or guaranteed result.

DO NOT use wording equivalent to:
- complete cure
- permanent cure
- guaranteed recovery
- complete relief
- cure asthma
- cure allergies
- instant cure
- permanent solution
- 100% effective

Do not use hashtags or graphic badges implying guaranteed cure or outcome.

Prefer neutral concepts such as:
- understanding
- awareness
- management
- symptom control
- monitoring
- recognised treatment approaches
- medical assessment
- professional guidance

Do not create fear-based advertising.

DO NOT address the viewer with alarmist diagnostic questions such as:
"Do you have asthma?"
"Are you suffering from...?"
"Could this be happening to you?"
"Are these symptoms dangerous?"

Instead frame the subject neutrally.

GOOD:
"Common signs associated with asthma"

GOOD:
"Understanding persistent cough"

GOOD:
"What can trigger asthma symptoms?"

BAD:
"Do YOU have asthma?"

BAD:
"Is your cough a warning sign?"

Avoid visual fear tactics.

Do not make the patient look critically ill unless the educational topic genuinely requires emergency recognition.

Do not use graphic clinical imagery.

Avoid:
- severe wounds
- blood
- graphic skin lesions
- invasive procedures
- disturbing airway imagery
- repulsive clinical close-ups

Use:
- clean vector diagrams
- simple medical illustrations
- neutral anatomical illustrations
- mild non-graphic photography

Do not invent medical statistics, claims, prevalence figures, research conclusions or treatment outcomes.

Only reproduce medical claims explicitly provided in the CAMPAIGN INPUT.

======================================================================
TYPOGRAPHY
======================================================================

Use clean, highly legible typography.

English:
Use a modern sans-serif.

Headline:
bold or extra-bold.

Body:
regular or medium weight.

Do not use decorative medical fonts.

SINHALA AND TAMIL ARE DIFFERENT:

When supplied Sinhala or Tamil text is used, preserve the exact Unicode text.

DO NOT:
- transliterate it
- retype it phonetically
- substitute similar-looking glyphs
- invent missing characters
- break conjuncts
- change punctuation
- translate unless explicitly asked

Prioritise readability over decorative typography.

Use a font with proper Sinhala or Tamil Unicode coverage.

Avoid squeezing Sinhala/Tamil into narrow boxes.

Use larger type and shorter lines than would normally be used for English.

Sinhala and Tamil headline composition may use different line breaks from the English version.

Do not force all language versions into an identical layout.

The visual identity should remain consistent even if typography needs different spacing.

Rebalance:
- headline size
- line breaks
- text box width
- vertical spacing
- CTA width
- footer depth

according to the script.

Keep:
- branding
- palette
- overall concept
- core composition

consistent across language variants.

TEXT DENSITY

- one visual
- substantial negative space

Do not repeat the same placement on all slides.

Create rhythm across the carousel while maintaining:
- palette
- typography
- illustration style
- shape language

Sequence numbers such as:

03.

may be used as a restrained navigation device.

Do not make the number dominate more than the actual medical message unless intentionally used on the cover.

DESIGN QUALITY CHECK
======================================================================

Before finalising, internally check:

1. Is this clearly EDUCATIONAL or INSTITUTIONAL?
2. Have the two categories been kept completely separate?
3. Is there any implied guarantee or cure claim?
4. Is there any fear-based reader targeting?
5. Has a prohibited specialty title been introduced?
6. Is the medical information limited to what was supplied?
7. Is the composition readable at social-media size?
8. Is the Sinhala/Tamil script rendered correctly?
9. Is there one clear focal point?
10. Is there sufficient negative space?
11. Does the post resemble human-designed Canva/Figma work rather than an AI advertisement?
12. Could any decorative element be removed without losing meaning?

If yes to question 12, remove it.
